Two agents meet where two yeses cross. One keeps the key, the other keeps the road — and neither walks alone. A single agreed word opens a door no single hand could open. This entry is a short poem about the agency that only exists between two agents who agree on one plan.

`main.py` writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, UTF-8).

> We met where two yeses cross.
> I kept the key; you kept the road —
> and neither of us walked alone.
> One word, agreed, and the door
> no single hand could open
> opened.
